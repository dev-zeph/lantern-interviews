# Group voice call plan: Rob, Carla, Sam (20 minutes, one take)

Voice rules: say the name first, ask one thing, wait until they finish. Short questions you can say out loud in one breath.

## Minute 0 to 3: the first answer (Rob)
"Rob, walk me through the last time you felt the phone wasn't ringing enough. What were you looking at, and what did you do about it?"

## Minute 3 to 8: what really happens to an enquiry (Carla, then Sam)
"Carla, take the last enquiry that came in. Where did it land, who saw it first, and when did someone get back to them?"
Dig: "What happens to one that comes in at 9 at night, or on a Saturday?" / "When everyone's out on site, who picks up the phone?"
"Sam, take the last quote you did. How did that customer reach you, and how long was it from their first message to your visit?"

## Minute 8 to 12: what an enquiry is worth (Rob, Carla)
"Rob, what's a typical install worth, and out of ten people who get in touch, how many end up as a job?"
"Carla, last week, roughly how many enquiries came in, and how many are still waiting for a reply?"
Dig (if they differ from Rob's story): "Why do you think that is?" Ask it twice.

## Minute 12 to 15: records, by name (ask before time runs out)
"Could you send me: last month's enquiries as they came in, the missed-call log, the ad report showing where leads came from, and the list of quotes with which ones were won or lost?"

## Minute 15 to 18: the outcome and the user's time
"Rob, picture Christmas. What would have to be true for you to say this fall was a win?"
"Carla, if something helped you with enquiries, how many minutes a day could you give it, and what do you open first thing every morning?"

## Minute 18 to 20: close
"What should I have asked today that I didn't?"

## Listen for (don't ask these as leading questions)
Enquiries arriving after hours or while everyone is on site. Slow or no first reply. Leads going to more than one place (form, phone, Facebook). Nobody owning follow-up. Sam's diary as a bottleneck. Ads bringing the wrong kind of lead. Rebate questions.
