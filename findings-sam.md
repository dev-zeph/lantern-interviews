## What we learned
- Small quotes go out the night of the visit. Big ones (ducting, several rooms, sizing and parts) are only written Sunday night at his kitchen table, so they go out 3 days to a week after the visit.
- Nobody contacts the customer between the visit and the quote, and nobody follows up after a quote, ever. A quote counts as lost when Lantern never hears back, so nobody knows why.
- Sam agrees from his own list: "the slow ones lose. People go with whoever sends a clear number first." From his seat he'd "look at the quotes before the ads." Rob decides. He asked not to be named or blamed in front of everyone.
- Works from his visit notes and measurements. Refused to share any quote or visit notes, even blanked out (private house details).
- Usability: no apps, no logins. Tried an app in the spring and never opened it ("Carla was fed up with me"). Wants new jobs by email, which he reads at lunch and at night.
- Routing rules (important for the auto-reply):
  - New install enquiries go to Sam for quotes; he wants all of them.
  - Repair or service on a Lantern unit goes to Carla, never Sam.
  - Repair on a unit Lantern didn't install: Lantern never takes these (warranty trouble in 2024). Carla sends a polite no.
- Viability: Carla runs info@ day to day. The website and anything technical is Rob's area. "Nobody's handing passwords around."
- 34 visits in Sept and Oct but 31 quotes on the list. He doesn't know why yet.

## Sam got back (same day)
- No template and no price list. Each big quote is priced from scratch from his visit notes and measurements: "a big part of why the big ones take a while."
- One big quote takes 1 to 1.5 hours. He fits 2 or 3 into a Sunday night.
- Booking email from Carla to Sam has the name, address, phone, what they want and the booked day. He reads it at lunch or at night.
- About 2 visits a day in Sept and Oct. Some days were full, some had room. Carla's sheet has the bookings.
- The 3 visits with no quote: one was just looking, one is waiting till spring, one sold the house. So the quote list is complete.
- Last big win: "ours was the first clear number she got", and she liked that he explained the ducting at the visit.
- At the visit he gives simple jobs a rough range on the spot. Big jobs get only "a few days", with no number.
- Rebate jobs are quoted the same way. The paperwork is Carla's.
- Spring app: the supplier pushed it, Carla set it up, and it didn't stick.
